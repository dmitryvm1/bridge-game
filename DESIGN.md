# Card Game Rules

## 1. Overview

The game is played with a traditional 36-card deck, containing cards from 6 through Ace.

**Number of players:** 2–4

**Objective:** Be the first to get rid of all the cards in your hand during each round and avoid accumulating penalty points. The game ends when a player's total score exceeds 125 points.

## 2. Dealing the Cards

1. One player acts as the dealer at the beginning of each round.
2. The dealer distributes cards one at a time, starting with the next player and proceeding clockwise.
3. Each player receives 5 cards, except the dealer, who receives 4.
4. The dealer places their fifth card face up on the table, starting the discard pile. This counts as the dealer's first move.
5. The remaining cards are placed face down next to the discard pile, forming the draw pile.

## 3. Turn Order and Basic Rules

Players take turns clockwise.

During their turn, a player may play one or more cards from their hand according to the following rules:

- A card can be played if it matches either the **suit or rank** of the top card on the discard pile.
- Multiple cards of the same rank can be played together in a single turn, even if they have different suits.
- Special cards may override the normal rules.

### When a Player Cannot Play

If a player has no cards that can legally be played:

1. They must draw one card from the draw pile.
2. If they can make a legal move after drawing, they may play.
3. Otherwise, their turn ends.

### When the Draw Pile Is Empty

If the draw pile runs out of cards:

1. Keep the top card of the discard pile face up on the table.
2. Shuffle all the remaining cards from the discard pile.
3. Place the shuffled cards face down to form a new draw pile.
4. Continue the game normally.

## 4. Special Cards

### 6 — Must Be Covered

A 6 must be covered by a card other than another 6.

The player who plays a 6 keeps their turn and must cover it. If they cannot legally cover the 6, they must continue drawing cards until they can play a valid card. This also applies to the dealer's opening card. Playing the last 6 in a hand does not end the round; it must still be covered.

The normal rule of drawing only one card does not apply in this situation.

### 7 — Draw One Card

The next player must draw 1 card from the draw pile.

### 8 — Draw Two Cards and Skip a Turn

The next player must draw 2 cards and skip their turn.

### Jack — Wild Card

A Jack can be played on any card, regardless of its suit or rank.

The player who plays the Jack chooses the suit that the next player must follow.

### Queen of Spades — Optional Rule

This special rule can be enabled or disabled in the game settings.

When enabled, playing the Queen of Spades causes the next player to:

- Draw 5 cards from the deck.
- Skip their turn.

### Ace — Skip a Turn

The next player must skip their turn.

## 5. Playing Multiple Special Cards

Multiple cards of the same rank can be played together.

When multiple 8s or Aces are played, their skip-turn effects are distributed among the following players, excluding the player who played them.

**Examples:**

- Two Aces: The next two players each skip one turn.
- Two 8s: The next two players each draw 2 cards and skip one turn.
- Two 8s in a two-player game: The opponent draws 4 cards and skips two consecutive turns.

### Special Card Penalties Cannot Be Stacked or Passed

A player cannot avoid, transfer, or increase a penalty by responding with another special card of the same rank.

For example, if a player is required to draw cards because of a 7 or 8, they must accept the penalty. They cannot play another 7 or 8 to pass the penalty to the next player.

## 6. Ending a Round

A round ends immediately when a player plays the last card or cards from their hand.

That player wins the round.

### Bonus for Finishing with Jacks

If the winning player finishes the round by playing one or more Jacks, they receive a score reduction of 20 points per Jack.

**Bonus = −20 × Number of Jacks played**

Examples:

- 1 Jack: −20 points
- 2 Jacks: −40 points
- 3 Jacks: −60 points

A player's cumulative score is allowed to become negative.

## 7. Scoring

At the end of each round, every player who still has cards in their hand calculates their penalty points.

These points are added to their cumulative score.

### Card Values

| Card | Penalty Points |
|---|---:|
| 6 | 0 |
| 7 | 0 |
| 8 | 0 |
| 9 | 0 |
| 10 | 10 |
| Jack | 20 |
| Queen | 10 |
| King | 10 |
| Ace | 15 |

### Scoring Example

A player finishes a round holding:

- One 8: 0 points
- One 10: 10 points
- One Queen: 10 points
- One Ace: 15 points

**Total penalty: 35 points**

These 35 points are added to the player's cumulative score.

## 8. Ending the Game

The game consists of multiple rounds, with scores accumulating after each round.

- If a player's cumulative score exceeds 125 points, the entire game ends immediately.
- The player who exceeds 125 points loses the game.
- If a player's score reaches exactly 125 points, their score resets to 0.
- Negative scores are allowed.

The objective is to avoid being the first player to exceed 125 points.
